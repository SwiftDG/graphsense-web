Before you upload

Use a chart GraphSense can read reliably.

  • Use a clear screenshot with one plotted line and visible axes.
  • Use the original chart where possible, not a photo taken at an angle.
  • Do not use charts with several lines, dense grids, or handwritten marks yet.
  • Check the written result against the original material before relying on it for coursework.

How it works

Built from a MATLAB research prototype.

GraphSense uses a browser version of the chart-analysis and sonification rules developed in MATLAB. The public beta processes images locally in your browser, so it does not require a MATLAB account or send your class materials to a server.
